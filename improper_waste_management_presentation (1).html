Section 01: The Topic

What is Improper Waste Management?

It refers to the improper disposal and handling of waste, such as throwing garbage on streets, in rivers, and in other public places instead of putting it in the proper trash bins.

Uncontrolled Disposal

Littering in streets, waterways & public hubs.

Neglected Bins

Bypassing designated waste containers.

Section 02: Background

Environmental Problem in Our Community

Improper waste management is one of the most widespread environmental challenges today. Many individuals discard trash carelessly without considering the immediate and long-term consequences on our surrounding ecosystem.

Environmental Pollution

Air, soil, and water degradation.

Clogged Drainages

Blocked waterways and gutters.

Severe Flooding

Urban overflows during storms.

Disease Outbreaks

Bacterial & mosquito vectors.

Cause & Effect Chain

Garbage Accumulation → Drainage Obstruction → Local Flooding

Section 03: Rationale

Why We Chose This Topic

We selected this critical topic because improper waste disposal directly impacts our immediate surroundings and public health. Action begins with community awareness.

Raise Awareness

Educating communities on correct waste disposal techniques, segregation, and reduction.

Promote Recycling & Reduction

Encouraging sustainable habits to minimize overall waste generation at the source.

Foster Civic Responsibility

Inspiring individuals to become proactive guardians of a cleaner, healthier environment.

Target Vision

Sustainable, Clean & Healthy Community
